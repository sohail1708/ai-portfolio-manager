📖 How it works

🎯 The experiment

$100,000 paper money on Alpaca. Every weekday at 21:00 BST (market close ET), a multi-agent LLM analyzes 9 NASDAQ tech stocks: AAPL · MSFT · NVDA · GOOGL · AMZN · META · TSLA · AVGO · ORCL. It rates each one on a 5-tier scale and places paper trades. Idle cash auto-parks in QQQ so we're never sitting in cash during a rally. After 30 days, we compare the portfolio's return to QQQ's. If we beat QQQ, the AI wins. If not, it lost.

⭐ The 5 ratings

Buy
Strong conviction long. Target 10% of NAV in this name.
Overweight
Tilt toward this above benchmark, but not aggressively. Target 5% of NAV. Means "constructive, accumulate on dips."
Hold
Neutral. No new action. Existing positions stay as-is.
Underweight
Tilt away. Trim 50% of current position if held.
Sell
Strong conviction bearish. Close the position entirely.

🤖 The 11 agents per ticker

For each stock, each weekday, 11 LLM agents debate before a final rating is issued:

  • 4 Analysts (parallel) — Market (technicals), Sentiment (Reddit/X), News (headlines), Fundamentals (10-Q/K)
  • 2 Researchers — Bull vs Bear debate over the analyst reports
  • 1 Research Manager — synthesizes the debate into an investment plan
  • 1 Trader — proposes a transaction with sizing + stop loss
  • 3 Risk Analysts — Risky / Neutral / Safe re-debate the proposal
  • 1 Portfolio Manager — issues the final 5-tier rating

Tap any ticker card to see all 11 agents' reasoning for that stock.

📊 Key terms

QQQ —
The Invesco QQQ ETF that tracks the NASDAQ-100 index. The benchmark we're trying to beat.
Alpha —
Outperformance vs the benchmark. +1% alpha means we beat QQQ by 1%.
NAV —
Net Asset Value. Total $ value of the portfolio: cash + value of all positions.
Price Target —
What the AI thinks the stock will reach within its stated horizon.
Horizon —
How long the AI expects to hold the position (typical: 3–18 months).
RSI —
Relative Strength Index. Above 70 = overbought; below 30 = oversold.

⚙️ Why this might fail

Beating QQQ for one month is a coin-flip even for professional managers. Most active strategies fail to beat the index over short windows. This is an honest experiment, not a guaranteed win. Either result is interesting: if AI wins, that's a portfolio piece. If it loses, "I gave an AI $100k and it still couldn't beat NASDAQ" is great content too.
